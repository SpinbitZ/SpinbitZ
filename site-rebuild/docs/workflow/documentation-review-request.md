## Review Criteria

### 1. Technical Accuracy
- Are all technical details correct?
- Do examples follow best practices?
- Is the architecture accurately described?
- Are testing approaches valid?

### 2. Completeness
- Are all required sections present?
- Are examples complete and runnable?
- Are all processes fully documented?
- Are there any gaps in coverage?

### 3. Clarity
- Is the documentation easy to understand?
- Are steps clear and sequential?
- Are examples relevant and helpful?
- Is the language appropriate?

### 4. Consistency
- Is formatting consistent?
- Are terms used consistently?
- Do examples follow the same style?
- Are processes aligned?

### 5. Practicality
- Can the team follow these guidelines?
- Are examples practical?
- Are processes implementable?
- Are standards achievable?
